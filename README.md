# flask_web_splitter
Web app for splitting things for many people, huge inspiration from the Split app. 
So the split app as the name suggests it splits your bills. When you have a bill and a lot of people you can keep track of your bills.
You have the Split tab where you can put your bills, price, number of people, what you already paid, and due date. Also if you input it wrong you can change it later. Then you have a search bar for when you have a lot of bills and you want to find it quickly. And you have a filter for all bills, paid bills, unpaid bills. 
Then the notes tab where you can write notes. Always usefull when you can write your ideas.
In the home page you have a draggable bills and notes with their informations. When you have a bill that has total amount value the same as already paid value it is taken as paid and the word paid will show on the card. 
Also you have the delete bill button and delete note button, which if you click will put the card in the bin page where you can either restore it if you clicked the delete button by mistake or delete it permanently. 
Then you have the statistics tab, which shows you your total expenses, what you already paid and reamaining. It is shows on the graph. Next to it you have the monthly expenses.
